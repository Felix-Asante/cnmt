# DESIGN.md — C.N Connect Customer Brag

## Style Prompt
Quiet premium remittance film on a clean white canvas: condensed navy headlines, sparse brand-red accents, soft brand-pink and navy radials, and crisp product UI cards with navy borders — confident, trustworthy, never flashy SaaS.

## Colors
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Soft wash: `#f7f8fa`
- Brand soft radial: `#fde8e9`
- Navy text: `#0a1a2f`
- Muted text: `#5a6577`
- Brand accent: `#d01018`
- Border: `#e2e6ee` (approx border)
- Gold optional: `#e8a317`

## Typography
- Display: Barlow Condensed, extrabold, uppercase for hero lines
- Body: Schibsted Grotesk, regular/medium

## Motion
- Soft power3.out entrances, 0.45–0.65s
- Soft crossfades between scenes
- Sequential UI rows/timeline steps with short stagger
- Subtle RMS-driven glow on brand accents only

## What NOT to Do
- No purple/indigo SaaS gradients
- No Inter/Roboto/system UI defaults as the hero type
- No dashboard/admin chrome
- No waveform/equalizer audio visuals
- No real customer PII — fictional stand-ins only
