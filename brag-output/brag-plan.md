# Brag Plan: C.N Connect (Customer)

## What is this app?

C.N.M.T app is the customer-facing international money transfer app — send from the UK/EU corridors to West and East Africa with clear rates, verified payouts, and delivery to mobile money or bank.

## The angle

Trust without the fog. Rates stay visible as you type, payment is verified before payout, and every transfer has a reference you can track. The video is a quiet, confident product film: show the send flow, then the “Delivered” beat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Giant display type: **FOR EVERYONE** in brand red, with **C.N.M.T** above in navy tracking. No corporate pitch — just the accent line from the real hero.

## Key moments (the middle)

- Corridor chip: 🇬🇧 GBP → GHS 🇬🇭 (from the real hero illustration)
- Transfer quote UI: “You send £250” / “They get GH₵…” with fee included
- Track timeline snapping: Payment → Verification → **Delivered**

## Outro / punchline

“Start a transfer in about a minute.” + **Send money** CTA + C.N Connect mark.

## User flow worth showing

1. **Entry** — Choose corridor and amount (UK → Ghana)
2. **Key action** — Confirm recipient / pay with a clear reference
3. **Result** — Track status lands on Delivered

## Tone

- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: quiet premium remittance product film
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, soft crossfades; brand red used sparingly as the accent; no jokes, no SaaS filler.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: `#ffffff` / soft wash `#f7f8fa` with brand-soft radial `#fde8e9`
- Accent: `#d01018` (brand)
- Text: `#0a1a2f` (navy); muted `#5a6577`
- Display font: Barlow Condensed
- Body font: Schibsted Grotesk
- Strongest visual element: hero phone mock + corridor chip + condensed uppercase headlines

## Share copy (draft)

Clear rates. Verified payouts. Money that actually arrives — C.N Connect.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm corporate bed with sparse UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (~115 BPM)
- Music treatment: start ~0s soft, hold under product scenes, fade under final logo (~0.8s)
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`; strong cues ~1.60s, 3.70s, 5.80s, 8.96s, 12.65s, 17.91s for major reveals; sequential card snaps on beat grid ~0.52s spacing
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; soft brand glow / card presence with RMS — no waveform visuals
- SFX posture: sparse; motion-matched interface taps for corridor chip, quote reveal, timeline step
- Audio-coupled moments: hook type/slam; corridor chip settle; timeline steps one-by-one; final logo hit
- Restraint rule: no aggressive whooshes; let the music carry polish

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.5s

Navy ground, soft brand radial. Eyebrow `C.N CONNECT`. Then slam `FOR EVERYONE` in brand red (Barlow Condensed extrabold uppercase).
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident open
Audio-coupled idea: title slam on strong cue ~1.60s
Music: warm bed in
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 3.5s

Hero claim from product copy: `International money transfer` with red accent `for everyone` still present as a whisper or integrated. Supporting line: clear rates, verified payouts, mobile money or bank. Corridor chip floats in: GBP → GHS.
Sequential/interaction: yes — chip settles after headline
Audio intent: product becomes concrete
Audio-coupled idea: soft chip settle SFX
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Quote — 5.0s

Recreate the transfer summary UI (fictional stand-ins): You send £250 · They get GH₵4,850 · Fee included · Ghana · MTN. Amounts tick/settle; Continue button present but not clicked.
Sequential/interaction: yes — you-send, they-get, fee rows appear one by one
Audio intent: precision / clarity
Audio-coupled idea: three soft UI ticks on beat grid
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Track — 5.0s

Track screen recreation: reference `TRANS-UK-GH-4821` (fictional). Timeline steps: Request submitted → Payment → Verification → **Payout complete / Delivered** with green settle.
Sequential/interaction: yes — timeline steps light one by one
Audio intent: payoff / relief
Audio-coupled idea: final Delivered on strong cue ~12.65–13.2s with soft confirmation chime
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 4.0s

`Fast money transfer at your convenience.` CTA pill `Send money` in brand red. Logo mark CN + C.N Connect. Hold.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: calm close; music fades
Audio-coupled idea: quiet logo hit
Transition mood: hold to black/end

**Music mood for this video:** polished / warm upbeat bed
**Audio summary:** Soft business bed under a restrained product demo; sparse UI ticks; music fades under the final mark.
