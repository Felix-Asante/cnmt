# Brag Plan: C.N Connect — Trust Loop Demo

## What is this app?
C.N Connect’s customer app lets people send money abroad with clear rates, a transferable reference, payment-proof verification before payout, status tracking, and cancel-before-pay control.

## The angle
“Money that moves with proof — clear rates in, verified payout out.”
A first-time-sender demo film: show the full trust loop, not a brand montage. Cancel is framed as control while unpaid — never failure.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Navy/white canvas. Eyebrow `C.N CONNECT`. Giant condensed line: **MONEY THAT MOVES WITH PROOF** (or split: MONEY THAT MOVES / WITH PROOF in brand red). Earn the next beat with clarity, not hype.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Live quote card: You send £250 · They get GH₵… · Fees included · Promo SAVE10 off what you pay
- Payment panel + upload proof drop zone (“Verified before payout”)
- Track timeline lighting to **Delivered**
- Cancel unpaid confirm → status **Cancelled** (calm, in-control)

## Outro / punchline
`Send money` CTA + “You’re in control before you pay.” + C.N Connect mark. Tiny support chip: WhatsApp / phone + reference.

## User flow worth showing
1. Send request (corridor → quote → recipient/promo → TRANS- reference + copy)
2. Pay + upload proof
3. Track to Delivered
4. Cancel unpaid (alternate trust beat — change of mind before payment)

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: finance-product demo film for first-time senders
- Interpretation: soft crossfades, sequential UI reveals, simulated taps; brand red sparingly; no jokes, no SaaS filler.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.5s
`C.N CONNECT` + **MONEY THAT MOVES WITH PROOF**. Sub: clear rates · verified payouts.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confident open
Audio-coupled idea: title settle ~1.6s
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Send request — 5.5s
Corridor chip GBP→GHS. Quote rows appear one-by-one (You send / They get / Fees / Promo SAVE10 · 10% off). Recipient “Ama Mensah · MTN”. Submit flash → reference `TRANS-UK-GH-4821` + Copy.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows then copy tap
Audio intent: precision
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Pay + proof — 5.0s
Navy amount-to-pay panel (£225.00 after promo) + reference. Account selected. Upload drop zone fills with “payment-proof.pdf”. Label: Verified before payout.
Sequential/interaction: yes — select account, then upload settle
Audio intent: trust building
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Track — 5.0s
Track transfer: enter reference → steps Request → Payment → Verification → **Delivered**. Home “Track transfer” CTA whisper.
Sequential/interaction: yes — steps light in order
Audio intent: relief / payoff
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Cancel (trust) — 4.0s
Unpaid transfer card. Ghost label “Before you pay”. Cancel confirm: optional reason “Wrong amount” → status **Cancelled**. Line: Change of mind? Cancel before payment.
Sequential/interaction: yes — confirm tap
Audio intent: calm control (not failure)
Transition mood: soft → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 3.0s
Mark + `Send money` CTA. Support chip. Tagline: You’re in control before you pay.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: fade out
Transition mood: hold end
