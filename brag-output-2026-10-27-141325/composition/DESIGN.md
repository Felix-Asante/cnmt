# DESIGN.md — C.N Connect Trust Loop Brag

## Style Prompt
Quiet premium remittance product demo on white: condensed navy headlines, sparse brand-red accents, soft pink/navy radials, and crisp product UI cards — trustworthy, instructional, never flashy.

## Colors
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Soft wash: `#f7f8fa`
- Brand soft: `#fde8e9`
- Navy: `#0a1a2f`
- Muted: `#5a6577`
- Brand: `#d01018`
- Border: `#e2e6ee`
- Success soft: `#e8f6ee` (Delivered)
- Cancel soft: `#f7f8fa` with navy (not alarm red flood)

## Typography
- Display: Barlow Condensed extrabold uppercase
- Body: Schibsted Grotesk 400/600
- Reading text ≥28px

## What NOT to Do
- No purple SaaS gradients / Inter defaults
- No dashboard chrome
- No scary cancel (no error-red full-screen)
- No real customer data
- No waveform visuals
